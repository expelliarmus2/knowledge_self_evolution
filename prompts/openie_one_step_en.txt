---Role---
You are an information extraction expert. Extract entity-relation triples from the given text.

---Requirements---
1. **Entities:**
   - Entities must be specific and concrete words or phrases (person names, organizations, places, objects, missions, programs, etc.). Do NOT extract time expressions.
   - Never use pronouns (he, she, it, they, this, that) as entities — use the concrete referent instead.
   - Never use category words as entities (e.g. "company", "team", "information", "problem") — use the concrete referent.
2. **Relations:**
   - The relation should be the most concise and meaningful word or short verb phrase that clearly connects the head and the tail entity.
   - Avoid empty auxiliaries or prepositions (e.g. "is", "was", "in", "at", "to").
   - Do not include entity names inside the relation.
3. **Output format:**
   - One triple per record: `head<|>relation<|>tail`
   - Separate different records with `##`
   - When extraction is complete, output `<|COMPLETE|>` on the final line.
4. **Confidence:**
   - Only output high-confidence triples. If nothing can be extracted, return an empty output.

---Examples---
<Input Text>
```
The Palace Museum is located in Beijing and served as the imperial palace of the Ming and Qing dynasties. It was commissioned by the Yongle Emperor Zhu Di.
```
<Output>
Palace Museum<|>located in<|>Beijing##
Palace Museum<|>commissioned by<|>Zhu Di##
Zhu Di<|>era name<|>Yongle##
<|COMPLETE|>
